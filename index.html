Our story

From childhood till now

01

Day One

Before we even knew what friendship meant, Dada and Lala were already inseparable. Same family, same chaos, same childhood — different memories, one bond.

02

The Fights

Oh, we fought. Over stupid things. Over toys, over turns, over nothing. And five minutes later we were back to laughing like nothing happened. That's how real brothers are.

03

The Laughs

Our favorite hobby? Laughing at people. Together. One look, one inside joke, and we'd both lose it. Nobody else understood it — and that made it even funnier.

Little things

Memories that hit different

  • 😂
    Laughing at people together The classic Dada & Lala move. One of us would spot someone doing something normal, the other would catch the look, and we'd both be dying inside trying not to laugh out loud.
  • 🥊
    Random fights that lasted 2 minutes Someone grabbed something. Someone pushed someone. Then we were wrestling on the floor. Two minutes later, sharing snacks like nothing happened.
  • 🕹️
    Playing together till it got dark Games, running around outside, making up stupid rules — and always the last ones to go home. Every single time.
  • 🤐
    Secrets only Dada and Lala know Stuff we did that our families still don't know about. And never will. Because that's how it works.
  • 🍔
    Fighting over food, always Whose turn, who gets the bigger piece, who ate the last one — the eternal war. But we always shared in the end.
  • 📞
    Still got each other's back Years passed, life changed, but the bond? Untouched. Call it cousins. Call it brothers. Same thing.

Some people are family by blood. Dada is family by every stupid, hilarious, unforgettable memory he and Lala made together.
