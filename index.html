Dada & Lala

Umer Hassan  ·  Muhammad Ibrahim

I'm Lala. This is Dada. We're cousins, but honestly — we're brothers. I made this page because I don't say this stuff out loud, and you know I don't. So here it is, in writing.

Scroll

Dada — you and I have been through everything together. Same childhood, same family gatherings, same absolutely stupid fights that ended in two minutes and then we were eating from the same plate again.

We laughed at people. Together. We'd catch each other's eyes in the middle of something and just lose it. Nobody else got the joke. That was the whole point.

So I made this. Not because it's your birthday. Not because anything happened. Just because you're Dada, and I'm Lala, and I don't think I've ever actually told you what that means to me.

So here it is. Read it. Don't make it weird.

— Lala

The stuff I remember

Things only we would get

  • 😂
    Laughing at people together Our #1 activity. We'd be somewhere normal, spot someone doing something normal, and one look between us was enough. Both of us fighting for our lives trying not to laugh. Dada knows exactly what I'm talking about.
  • 🥊
    The fights that lasted 2 minutes You grabbed something. I grabbed it back. Next thing we're rolling on the floor. Then 2 minutes later we're sharing snacks like nothing happened. Every single time. I don't even remember what most of those fights were about.
  • 🕹️
    Playing outside till it got dark Making up rules, changing them mid-game when one of us was losing, arguing over who cheated. And always, always, the last ones to go home. Every single day.
  • 🤐
    The stuff nobody knows Things Dada and I did that our families will never find out about. And I'm keeping it that way. If you're reading this, Dada — you know which ones. Take them to the grave.
  • 🍔
    The food wars Whose turn. Who got the bigger piece. Who ate the last one. We fought over food like it was our job. But somehow — we always split it in the end.
  • 📞
    Still the same Years went by. Life got busy. But when I call Dada, it's like no time passed at all. Same jokes. Same nonsense. Same brother. That's the thing that never changes.

Most people are just cousins. Dada is the guy I'd fight anyone for, and I know he'd do the same for me.

— Lala
